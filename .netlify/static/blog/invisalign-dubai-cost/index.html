Invisalign in Dubai 2026: Cost, Clinics & What to Expect

How much does Invisalign cost in Dubai? Prices from AED 8,000 to AED 25,000. Compare certified providers and learn what affects pricing.

Invisalign Cost in Dubai

Invisalign clear aligners have become the most popular orthodontic treatment in Dubai, offering a discreet alternative to traditional braces. Here's everything you need to know about pricing and finding the right provider.

Price Guide

|----------------|-------------------|----------|----------|

Invisalign Express (minor cases)8,000 – 12,0003-6 monthsUp to 7
Invisalign Comprehensive (complex)18,000 – 25,00012-24 monthsUnlimited
Invisalign Teen15,000 – 22,00012-18 monthsUnlimited

What's Included in the Price?

Most Invisalign quotes in Dubai include:

  • Initial consultation and 3D scanning
  • All aligner sets for the treatment plan
  • Attachments (tooth-colored bumps) if needed
  • Regular check-up appointments (every 6-8 weeks)
  • First set of retainers after treatment

    • Watch out for hidden costs:

  • Additional aligners if treatment extends beyond the plan
  • Replacement retainers (AED 500-1,500 per set)
  • IPR (interproximal reduction) if needed

    How to Choose an Invisalign Provider

  • Look for certified providers — Invisalign ranks providers by experience (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond). Higher tiers mean more cases completed.
  • Check before/after photos — Ask to see results from cases similar to yours
  • 3D treatment preview — Any good provider will show you a digital simulation of your expected results before you commit
  • Payment plans — Many clinics offer 0% interest installments over 6-12 months

    • Best Areas for Orthodontic Treatment

    Dubai Healthcare City has a concentration of orthodontic specialists. Downtown, Dubai Marina, and Jumeirah also have many certified Invisalign providers.
