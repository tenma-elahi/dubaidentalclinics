<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Common Dental Emergencies & First Aid</h3>
<h4 class="text-lg font-semibold mt-5 mb-2 text-gray-900">Knocked-Out Tooth</h4>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Time is critical — you have about 30 minutes.</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Pick up the tooth by the crown (white part), never the root</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">If dirty, rinse gently with milk or saline — don't scrub</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Try to place it back in the socket and bite down gently on a cloth</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">If you can't reinsert it, keep it in milk or between your cheek and gum</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Get to a dentist within 30 minutes for the best chance of saving the tooth</li>
<h4 class="text-lg font-semibold mt-5 mb-2 text-gray-900">Severe Toothache</h4>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Rinse with warm salt water</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Take ibuprofen (NOT aspirin, which can increase bleeding)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Apply a cold compress to the outside of your cheek</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Don't place aspirin directly on the gum — this causes chemical burns</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">See a dentist as soon as possible</li>
<h4 class="text-lg font-semibold mt-5 mb-2 text-gray-900">Broken or Chipped Tooth</h4>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Rinse your mouth with warm water</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">If bleeding, apply gauze for 10 minutes</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Save any broken pieces</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Cover sharp edges with dental wax or sugar-free gum temporarily</li>
<h4 class="text-lg font-semibold mt-5 mb-2 text-gray-900">Lost Crown or Filling</h4>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">If you have the crown, try to slip it back over the tooth using denture adhesive or toothpaste as temporary cement</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Don't use regular glue</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Avoid chewing on that side</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">See a dentist within 24-48 hours</li>
<h4 class="text-lg font-semibold mt-5 mb-2 text-gray-900">Abscess (Swelling with Pus)</h4>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>This is serious — infection can spread.</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Rinse with mild salt water several times a day</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Take over-the-counter pain medication</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">Don't try to drain it yourself</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal">See a dentist or visit an emergency room immediately</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">When to Go to the Hospital Instead</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Go to the nearest hospital emergency room (not a dental clinic) if:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">You have <strong>uncontrolled bleeding</strong> that won't stop after 20 minutes of pressure</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">You have <strong>facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing</strong></li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">You have a <strong>jaw fracture</strong> (unable to open or close your mouth)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">You've had <strong>trauma to the face/head</strong> with possible concussion</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Major hospitals with dental emergency support:</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Dubai Health Authority hospitals (Rashid Hospital, Dubai Hospital)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Mediclinic City Hospital</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">American Hospital Dubai</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Insurance Coverage for Dental Emergencies</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Most Dubai health insurance plans cover emergency dental treatment, including:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Emergency consultation and X-rays</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Emergency extractions</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Treatment to relieve acute pain</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Check your policy for:</strong> Annual limits, co-payment amounts, and whether you need pre-authorization. In a true emergency, most insurers allow you to seek treatment first and file claims later.</p>
