<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Zirconia vs. E-max: Which Is Better?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Zirconia crowns</strong> are extremely strong and ideal for back teeth or patients who grind their teeth. They've improved aesthetically in recent years but may still look slightly less natural than E-max on front teeth.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>E-max crowns</strong> offer the best aesthetics — they're translucent like natural teeth and excellent for front teeth. They're strong enough for most situations but may chip under heavy grinding.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Our recommendation:</strong> E-max for front teeth (appearance matters most), Zirconia for back teeth (strength matters most).</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Same-Day Crowns (CEREC)</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Several Dubai clinics now offer CEREC same-day crowns. Instead of two visits with a temporary crown, the entire process — scanning, milling, and bonding — is completed in a single appointment (usually 2-3 hours).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Pros:</strong> No temporary crown, no second visit, digital precision</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Cons:</strong> Slightly higher cost, limited material options (usually E-max or zirconia blocks)</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">When Do You Need a Crown?</h3>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">After a root canal treatment (especially on molars)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">For a cracked or severely broken tooth</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">To cover a large filling when little natural tooth remains</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Over a dental implant</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">To improve the appearance of a misshapen or discolored tooth</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">As part of a dental bridge</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">How Long Do Crowns Last?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">With proper care, dental crowns last:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Metal/PFM:</strong> 10-15 years</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Full porcelain:</strong> 10-15 years</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>E-max:</strong> 15-20 years</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Zirconia:</strong> 15-20+ years</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Key factors:</strong> oral hygiene, grinding habits, diet, and regular dental check-ups.</p>
