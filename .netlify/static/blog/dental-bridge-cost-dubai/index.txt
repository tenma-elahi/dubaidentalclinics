<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">How Does a Dental Bridge Work?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">A traditional dental bridge works by:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Preparing</strong> the teeth on either side of the gap (grinding them down for crowns)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Taking impressions</strong> or digital scans</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Placing a temporary bridge</strong> while the lab makes the permanent one</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Cementing the permanent bridge</strong> 1-2 weeks later</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">The entire process typically requires 2-3 visits over 2-3 weeks.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Bridge vs. Implant: Which Is Better?</h3>

<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Choose a bridge if:</strong> You want a faster, non-surgical option, have healthy adjacent teeth that could benefit from crowns anyway, or aren't a candidate for implants.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Choose an implant if:</strong> You want the longest-lasting solution, don't want to modify healthy teeth, or are replacing a single tooth with healthy neighbors.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">When Is a Bridge Not Recommended?</h3>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Adjacent teeth are weak or have large fillings (can't support the bridge)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Multiple consecutive teeth are missing (3+ in a row)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">You grind your teeth heavily (high failure risk)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">The gap has been present for years and bone has receded significantly</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Caring for Your Dental Bridge</h3>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Floss under the bridge daily</strong> — Use a floss threader, superfloss, or water flosser</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Brush twice daily</strong> — Pay extra attention to where the bridge meets the gum</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Avoid hard and sticky foods</strong> — Can dislodge or damage the bridge</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Regular dental checkups</strong> — Every 6 months to check the bridge and surrounding teeth</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Night guard if you grind</strong> — Protects the bridge from excessive force</li>
