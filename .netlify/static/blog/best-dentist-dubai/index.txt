2:T1385,<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mt-8 mb-4 text-gray-900">How to Find the Best Dentist in Dubai</h2>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">With over 700 dental clinics across Dubai, finding the right dentist can feel overwhelming. Whether you need a routine checkup or a complex procedure, here's how to find a top-rated dentist in your area.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">What Makes a Great Dentist in Dubai?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>DHA Licensing</strong> — Every practicing dentist in Dubai must be licensed by the Dubai Health Authority. This ensures minimum qualification standards, but the best dentists go beyond the minimum with additional certifications and continuous education.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Patient Reviews</strong> — Look for dentists with consistent 4.5+ star ratings across Google and other platforms. Pay attention to recent reviews (last 6-12 months) for the most accurate picture.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Specialization</strong> — General dentists handle routine care, but for specific procedures (implants, orthodontics, cosmetic work), seek out specialists with focused training.</p>
