<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">What's Usually Included in the Price</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Most orthodontic packages in Dubai include:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Initial consultation and X-rays/scans</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">The braces or aligners themselves</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">All adjustment appointments (monthly for braces)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Retainers after treatment (1 set)</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Often not included:</strong> Additional aligners (refinements), replacement retainers, extraction of teeth before treatment, and emergency visits for broken brackets.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Metal Braces: The Budget Option</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Traditional metal braces remain the most affordable and effective option. Modern brackets are much smaller and more comfortable than they were 10 years ago. They're ideal for:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Complex cases requiring significant tooth movement</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Children and teenagers</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Budget-conscious patients</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Cases requiring jaw correction</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Invisalign vs. Other Clear Aligners</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Invisalign</strong> is the most well-known clear aligner brand, but it's not the only option. Competitors like ClearCorrect, Spark, and SureSmile offer similar technology, sometimes at lower prices.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Key differences:</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Invisalign:</strong> Most established, widest range of case complexity, premium price</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>ClearCorrect:</strong> Good for mild-moderate cases, often 20-30% cheaper</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Spark:</strong> Newer material that resists staining better</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>In-house aligners:</strong> Some clinics make their own aligners with 3D printers — quality varies</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Who's a Good Candidate for Clear Aligners?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Clear aligners work well for:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Mild to moderate crowding</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Spacing issues</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Minor bite corrections</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Adults who want a discreet option</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Not ideal for:</strong> Severe crowding, significant bite issues, cases requiring tooth rotation over 20 degrees, or patients who won't wear them 22 hours/day.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Children vs. Adult Orthodontics</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Children (7-12):</strong> Early intervention (Phase 1) with expanders or partial braces. Cost: AED 5,000-10,000.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Teenagers (12-18):</strong> Full braces or Invisalign Teen. Most common age for orthodontic treatment. Cost: AED 5,000-20,000.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Adults (18+):</strong> All options available. Increasing trend toward clear aligners. Treatment may take longer due to denser bone. Cost: AED 8,000-30,000.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">How to Choose an Orthodontist in Dubai</h3>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Verify they're a specialist</strong> — an orthodontist has 2-3 years additional training beyond dental school</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Ask about their experience with your specific case type</strong></li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Request a treatment plan with timeline</strong> before committing</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Compare at least 3 clinics</strong> — prices vary significantly</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Ask about payment plans</strong> — most clinics offer 0% installments over 6-12 months</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Check reviews specifically for orthodontic work</strong></li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Payment Plans & Insurance</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Most Dubai dental clinics offer interest-free payment plans for orthodontic treatment:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Typical structure:</strong> 30-50% upfront, remainder in monthly installments</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Insurance:</strong> Many plans cover orthodontics for children under 18 (AED 5,000-10,000 lifetime max)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Adult orthodontics:</strong> Rarely covered by insurance in Dubai</li>
