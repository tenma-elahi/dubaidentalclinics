Braces Cost in Dubai 2026: Metal, Ceramic, Lingual & Clear Aligners

Complete guide to orthodontic treatment costs in Dubai. Compare metal braces, ceramic braces, lingual braces, and clear aligners like Invisalign.

How Much Do Braces Cost in Dubai?

Orthodontic treatment in Dubai ranges from AED 5,000 to AED 30,000 depending on the type of braces, complexity of your case, and the clinic. Treatment typically takes 12-24 months.

What's Usually Included in the Price

Most orthodontic packages in Dubai include:

  • Initial consultation and X-rays/scans
  • The braces or aligners themselves
  • All adjustment appointments (monthly for braces)
  • Retainers after treatment (1 set)

    • Often not included: Additional aligners (refinements), replacement retainers, extraction of teeth before treatment, and emergency visits for broken brackets.

    Metal Braces: The Budget Option

    Traditional metal braces remain the most affordable and effective option. Modern brackets are much smaller and more comfortable than they were 10 years ago. They're ideal for:

  • Complex cases requiring significant tooth movement
  • Children and teenagers
  • Budget-conscious patients
  • Cases requiring jaw correction

    • Invisalign vs. Other Clear Aligners

    Invisalign is the most well-known clear aligner brand, but it's not the only option. Competitors like ClearCorrect, Spark, and SureSmile offer similar technology, sometimes at lower prices.

    Key differences:

  • Invisalign: Most established, widest range of case complexity, premium price
  • ClearCorrect: Good for mild-moderate cases, often 20-30% cheaper
  • Spark: Newer material that resists staining better
  • In-house aligners: Some clinics make their own aligners with 3D printers — quality varies

    • Who's a Good Candidate for Clear Aligners?

    Clear aligners work well for:

  • Mild to moderate crowding
  • Spacing issues
  • Minor bite corrections
  • Adults who want a discreet option

    • Not ideal for: Severe crowding, significant bite issues, cases requiring tooth rotation over 20 degrees, or patients who won't wear them 22 hours/day.

    Children vs. Adult Orthodontics

    Children (7-12): Early intervention (Phase 1) with expanders or partial braces. Cost: AED 5,000-10,000.

    Teenagers (12-18): Full braces or Invisalign Teen. Most common age for orthodontic treatment. Cost: AED 5,000-20,000.

    Adults (18+): All options available. Increasing trend toward clear aligners. Treatment may take longer due to denser bone. Cost: AED 8,000-30,000.

    How to Choose an Orthodontist in Dubai

  • Verify they're a specialist — an orthodontist has 2-3 years additional training beyond dental school
  • Ask about their experience with your specific case type
  • Request a treatment plan with timeline before committing
  • Compare at least 3 clinics — prices vary significantly
  • Ask about payment plans — most clinics offer 0% installments over 6-12 months
  • Check reviews specifically for orthodontic work

    • Payment Plans & Insurance

    Most Dubai dental clinics offer interest-free payment plans for orthodontic treatment:

  • Typical structure: 30-50% upfront, remainder in monthly installments
  • Insurance: Many plans cover orthodontics for children under 18 (AED 5,000-10,000 lifetime max)
  • Adult orthodontics: Rarely covered by insurance in Dubai
