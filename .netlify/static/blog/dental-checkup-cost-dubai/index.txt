<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">What's Included in a Standard Checkup?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">A good dental checkup should include:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Visual examination</strong> — The dentist checks all teeth, gums, tongue, and soft tissues</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Digital X-rays</strong> — Usually 2 bitewing X-rays to check for cavities between teeth (full mouth X-rays annually or for new patients)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Gum assessment</strong> — Checking for signs of gingivitis or periodontal disease</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Oral cancer screening</strong> — Visual and tactile examination of soft tissues</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-decimal"><strong>Treatment plan</strong> — Discussion of any issues found and recommended treatments</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">How Often Should You Get a Checkup?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Standard recommendation:</strong> Every 6 months for most adults and children.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>More frequent visits (every 3-4 months) if you:</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Have a history of gum disease</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Are prone to cavities</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Smoke or use tobacco</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Have diabetes</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Are pregnant</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Wear braces</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Less frequent (annually) if you:</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Have excellent oral health</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">No history of cavities or gum disease</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Practice good home care</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Professional Cleaning: Is It Worth Adding?</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Yes. Professional cleaning (scaling and polishing) removes:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Plaque</strong> — Soft bacterial film that causes cavities</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Tartar/calculus</strong> — Hardened plaque that can't be removed by brushing</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Surface stains</strong> — From coffee, tea, or food</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Cost:</strong> AED 200-500 for a standard cleaning, AED 500-1,500 for deep cleaning (if gum disease is present).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Tip:</strong> Book a "checkup + cleaning" package — most clinics offer a better price than booking separately.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Insurance Coverage</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Almost all Dubai health insurance plans cover preventive dental care:</p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>2 checkups per year</strong> — typically 80-100% covered</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>Annual cleaning</strong> — usually included</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc"><strong>X-rays</strong> — covered as part of diagnostic care</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Without insurance:</strong> Many clinics offer membership plans (AED 500-1,500/year) that include 2 checkups, cleanings, X-rays, and discounts on treatments. These can save 30-50% compared to paying per visit.</p>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">What to Look for in a Checkup Clinic</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Red flags:</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Pushing expensive treatments without clear explanation</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Not taking X-rays (can't see cavities between teeth without them)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Rushing through the exam (a thorough check takes 15-20 minutes minimum)</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">No discussion of findings or treatment options</li>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Green flags:</strong></p>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Takes time to explain findings with visual aids</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Shows you X-rays and explains what they see</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Discusses prevention, not just treatment</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Provides a written treatment plan with cost estimates</li>
<li class="ml-4 mb-1 text-gray-700 list-disc">Doesn't pressure you into same-day treatment</li>
<h3 class="text-xl font-bold mt-6 mb-3 text-gray-900">Affordable Checkups by Area</h3>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Best value areas:</strong> Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Nahda, International City, and Al Qusais. Many clinics in these areas offer checkup + cleaning packages under AED 400.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>University clinics:</strong> Dubai's dental schools and training programs sometimes offer reduced-cost checkups performed by supervised students or residents.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong>Promotional offers:</strong> Check clinic websites and social media for "new patient" offers — many clinics offer free first checkups or heavily discounted packages.</p>
