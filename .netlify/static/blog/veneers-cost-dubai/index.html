Dental Veneers Cost in Dubai 2026: Porcelain, Composite & Lumineers

How much do veneers cost in Dubai? Compare porcelain, composite, and Lumineer prices. Find the best clinics for your Hollywood smile.

How Much Do Dental Veneers Cost in Dubai?

Dental veneers in Dubai cost between AED 800 and AED 6,000 per tooth, depending on the type of veneer and the clinic. A full set of veneers (8-10 teeth for a "Hollywood smile") typically costs AED 15,000 to AED 50,000.

Porcelain vs. Composite Veneers

Composite veneers are applied directly to the teeth in a single visit. They're more affordable and require less tooth preparation, but they stain more easily and need replacing sooner.

Porcelain veneers are custom-made in a lab and bonded to your teeth over two visits. They look more natural, resist staining, and last much longer — making them the better long-term value despite the higher upfront cost.

What Are Lumineers?

Lumineers are ultra-thin porcelain veneers (0.2mm vs. 0.5mm for traditional porcelain). Their main advantage is minimal or no tooth preparation — your natural tooth structure stays intact. However, they may not work for severely discolored or misaligned teeth.

The "Hollywood Smile" Package

Many Dubai clinics offer Hollywood smile packages that include:

  • Full consultation and digital smile design
  • 8-20 porcelain or E-max veneers
  • Teeth whitening for remaining teeth
  • All follow-up appointments

    • Package prices typically range from AED 20,000 to AED 60,000, often with payment plans available.

    How to Choose the Right Veneers

  • Be realistic about your budget — composite veneers are fine for a trial run
  • Ask to see before/after photos of the dentist's actual patients
  • Request a digital smile preview (DSD) before committing
  • Understand the prep involved — some veneers require shaving down your teeth permanently
  • Check the lab — ask which dental lab makes the veneers (local vs. international)
  • Get a detailed written quote that includes all visits, the temporary set, and any adjustments

    • Maintenance Tips

  • Avoid biting hard foods directly with veneered teeth
  • Don't use teeth as tools (opening packages, etc.)
  • Maintain regular dental hygiene
  • Visit your dentist every 6 months
  • Avoid excessive coffee, tea, and red wine (especially with composite veneers)
